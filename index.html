How to read this

A positive result means the observed interval is above zero and the two-sided p-value is below alpha = 0.05. An inconclusive result means the observed data does not distinguish the effect from zero under this approximation. It does not mean the variants are identical.

Do not ship from the primary metric alone. Check instrumentation, randomization, exposure, task mix, guardrails, practical effect size, and whether the test design matches the assumptions.
